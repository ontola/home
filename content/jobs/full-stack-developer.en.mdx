## What we are looking for

We are looking for a tech-savvy, eager-to-learn developer with a passion for open data and building robust applications.

- You speak **Dutch**
- Experience as a **Full-stack developer**
- You are **not afraid of new technology**
- You like to **build projects from start to finish**
- You **dare to use AI** in your daily workflow
- You have **good taste in UI / UX**, and an affinity with software and ease of use
- You have a clear **opinion on elegant and maintainable architecture**

- Experience with our stack: **Rust** + **Typescript** + **React**
- You have an **enterprising / intrapreneur** mindset
- You have solid **Project management skills**
- Affinity with _knowledge management, table editors, text editors_

- Affinity with _radio, IOT, or development boards_.
