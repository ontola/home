## Where did my disk space go?

SpaceMan shows where your space goes, suggests what can safely be removed and lets you mark things as "do not touch".

<BigImage image="cases/spaceman_treemap.png" alt="SpaceMan on macOS, showing a treemap of a folder" />

## Features

- **Explore**: drill down through folders, largest first, as a list or as a treemap. Results appear while the scan is still running.
- **Clean up**: suggestions for caches, build output (`node_modules`, Rust `target`, Xcode DerivedData and more), logs, the Trash, old downloads and very large files. Nothing is ticked for you.
- **Do not touch**: protect any file or folder. Protected items are never suggested and cannot be removed.
- **Undo**: removed items go to the Trash.

## Fast, and cached

The scanner walks the disk in parallel and measures the real size on disk.
A warm scan of a tree with 237,000 files takes about 0.2 seconds, roughly five times faster than `du`.
Results are cached, so the last scan shows instantly on startup and refreshes in the background.

## Technology

SpaceMan is a Flutter app on top of a Rust core.
macOS comes first; Windows, Linux, iOS and Android share the same code.
It is in active development, with unsigned builds for macOS, Windows and Android.
