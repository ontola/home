## Where did my disk space go?

SpaceMan shows where your space goes, suggests what can safely be removed and lets you mark things as "do not touch".

<BigImage image="cases/spaceman_devices.webp" alt="SpaceMan on a laptop, showing a treemap of a folder, and in its phone layout, showing the same folder as a list" />

## On your computer and your phone

SpaceMan runs on macOS, Windows and Android, from one codebase.
On a phone you get the same app in a layout made for one hand: a list instead of a sidebar, and Explore, Clean up and Do not touch as tabs at the bottom.

## Features

- **Explore**: drill down through folders, largest first, as a list or as a treemap. Results appear while the scan is still running.
- **Clean up**: suggestions for caches, build output (`node_modules`, Rust `target`, Xcode DerivedData and more), logs, the Trash, old downloads and very large files. Nothing is ticked for you.
- **Do not touch**: protect any file or folder. Protected items are never suggested and cannot be removed.
- **Undo**: on the desktop, removed items go to the Trash.

## Fast, and cached

The scanner walks the disk in parallel and measures the real size on disk.
A warm scan of a tree with 237,000 files takes about 0.2 seconds, roughly five times faster than `du`.
Results are cached, so the last scan shows instantly on startup and refreshes in the background.

## Technology

SpaceMan is a Flutter app on top of a Rust core.
macOS came first; Windows, Linux, iOS and Android share the same code.
It is in active development.

- [Source code on GitHub](https://github.com/ontola/spaceman)

## Download

- [Download for macOS](https://github.com/ontola/spaceman/releases/latest/download/SpaceMan-macOS.zip): unzip, move SpaceMan to Applications, and the first time right-click it and choose *Open*.
- [Download for Windows](https://github.com/ontola/spaceman/releases/latest/download/SpaceMan-Windows.zip): unzip and run `SpaceMan.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- [Download for Android](https://github.com/ontola/spaceman/releases/latest/download/SpaceMan-Android.apk): open the APK on your phone and allow installing from this source.

The builds are not signed yet. All versions are on the [releases page](https://github.com/ontola/spaceman/releases).
