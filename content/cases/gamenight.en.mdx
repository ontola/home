## Less setup, more playing together

Playing multiplayer games with friends should feel like listening to a playlist.
You don't stop after every song to reconfigure your speakers, so why stop after every game to browse a library, invite everyone again and remap controllers?

GameNight keeps the party together while the games come and go.
Pick a game, play a round, try something else.
The next game loads in the background while you play, and your controllers stay assigned to the same players across games.

## A whole evening of games

A game night needs games, so we made them ourselves.
The catalog has over a dozen free, open-source games built for GameNight: hover racing in SpaceRacer, party football in Ballkickers, grappling, blasting frogs in Frog Fighter, an arena shooter where the loser of each round picks the upgrade in Growing Guns, and eight quick party games like Blast Party and Volley Trouble.
We also brought Mineclonia, an open-source voxel sandbox, to GameNight with splitscreen.

## Your player comes with you

Scan the QR code in the lobby to open the character editor on your phone.
Draw a face, choose your colours and change the playlist.
Integrated games can reuse your name, colours and artwork, so your player shows up in every game of the evening.

## Open protocol, open source

The local runtime works without an account or a cloud service.
You can run it, change it and add your own games.
Games integrate through an open protocol, with SDKs for Rust, Godot and C/C++, or by implementing the JSON protocol directly.
Independent developers can publish their games in the [GameNight catalog](https://gamenight.ontola.io/catalog).

GameNight is an early preview for Windows and macOS.
